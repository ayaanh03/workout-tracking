# Global fatigue and observation

Trigger: [adjustments](adjustments.md#global-fatigue). Keep one persistent flag with response dates, saved normal doses, observation doses and actual evidence by modality in [recovery state](../state/recovery.json). Apply to unperformed sessions only.

## While active

- Freeze load/effort/stage increases, forearm increases, run pace/minutes, bike duration and added-set trials. Reductions remain allowed. The prescribed recovery-to-observation transition is allowed.
- Defer singles and 5K tests. Suspend final 0–1 sets during recovery/observation: use stage RIR, or 1–2 for those last sets if otherwise D.
- Cancel unresolved added-set trials before calculating reduced work. Previously adopted additions remain in the normal snapshot unless local rules remove them. Cancelled trials/harder last-set targets do not return automatically.
- Clear progression counters and cancel carried pace adjustments on entering Option C. Keep credit inactive; recovery sessions alone cannot clear the flag.

## Seven-day recovery

From the next unperformed session, use [Option C](assessments.md) for a full seven days: [reduced lifting](ramp-and-return.md#reduced-lifting--w10-or-option-c), no hard runs/tests, comfortable easy cycling only when appropriate. Undo an implicated recent discretionary increase under the local rules. Use one reduction, not overlapping halvings.

At reassessment:

1. Continuing reported/observed fatigue, worsening symptoms or poor day-to-day function: another seven days of Option C, review cause, then reassess.
2. Missing necessary report: hold current phase/restrictions pending evidence; no automatic new seven-day period.
3. Acceptable recovery: begin a full seven-day observation at the doses below, flag still active.

## Observation doses

- Lifts: lower of last clearly tolerated stage and saved illness/gap return limit, with all local rollbacks. Without a clear tolerated dose, B only if previously tolerated and currently appropriate; otherwise A. Symptoms can reduce further.
- Runs: applicable [return level](run-return.md); no prior normal success means calibration. Any remaining restoration waits for clearance.
- Bikes: no more than last clearly tolerated duration or current calendar ceiling.
- Planned assessment caps (early assessment/W5/W10) still apply. Those capped weeks cannot supply the normal-dose observation week. After they end, the transition to the appropriate observation dose is expressly allowed while flagged. Tests remain deferred.

## Clearance

Keep the flag active throughout observation. Clear only after a **complete performed observation week at appropriate doses**, acceptable performance/recovery, no worsening symptoms and no continuing decline in priority sessions. Elapsed time, skipped sessions or merely lacking comparable decline evidence is insufficient; continue observation until needed work is observed.

At clearance, reset counters and begin from doses actually tolerated. Introduce only **one substantive progression or restoration at a time**, observe its response, then consider another. Retain unfinished run-return limits and persistent local cuts. Do not automatically restore old calendar doses, removed sets or near-failure effort. Record individual decline-case status separately at this review.
