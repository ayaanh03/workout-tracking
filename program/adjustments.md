# Symptoms, evidence and local adjustments

Use dated reports in [current state](../Tracker.md), with the decision and source actuals retained in history. Record lift-set load/reps/RIR; run-rep pace, final RPE and next-morning recovery; bike minutes/effort. Weekly review each run's pace, minutes, format, effort/recovery and resulting hold/slower/faster/next-level decision. Ramp/return/observation weeks also need a manageable-week answer.

## Missing evidence

Ask once in a compact prompt for decision-critical missing recovery/metrics at the next regular interaction. Silence means unknown, never clean, normal, manageable or poor. A completed session may have pending classification.

- Block only the advancement, restoration or clearance needing that evidence; otherwise repeat the current appropriate dose.
- Ordinary run recovery still unknown at its next corresponding run: mark the old session no-count; clear ordinary and comfortable credit and pending carried credit. No poor-recovery strike, no later reconstructed award. Assessment recovery follows [run progression](run-progression.md).
- Return/observation validation remains pending until appropriate work and recovery are observed. Do not restore unvalidated work.
- Missing manageable-week answer holds stage. A later explicitly manageable completed week can release the hold; calendar rollover cannot.
- Unknown global recovery evidence holds the current phase, not a new seven-day clock or automatic clearance.

Corrections affect records/future decisions; never rewrite completed prescriptions or make up missed work.

## Compare performance

A comparable lift uses the same slot, load, setup, range, rest and first-set RIR. Ordinary progression/plateau comparisons additionally require the full same set count/effort targets. Ramp, reduced, return, illness-affected, heavy-single, changed-equipment or temporarily shortened work does not establish an ordinary plateau. A rep drop after adding load is not automatically decline.

- **Progress:** more clean reps across the same sets at comparable effort, or a successful load increase. Later-set progress counts.
- **Stable plateau:** three comparable normal exposures without progress and no downward trend. Hold and review; no automatic extra sets.
- **Fixed reference:** before a set/effort/interfering-dose trial, save the most recent comparable normal first set. Keep it fixed while evaluating. If unavailable, first establish a normal anchor at the current load/first-set RIR and collect comparisons. Later-set changes do not erase this first-set reference; load/first-set RIR changes require a new anchor.
- **Persistent decline:** first set ≥2 clean reps below reference on two consecutive comparable exposures, or cumulative ≥2-rep decline across three comparable exposures (`10→8→8` or `10→9→8`). Compare RIR/execution before judging deterioration. Decline never earns extra sets.

## Local response

Pain that worsens or changes execution/gait requires stopping, reducing or modifying the aggravating movement immediately. Persistent/worsening symptoms warrant assessment. Do not wait for a counter. Ordinary performance rollbacks below require **persistent decline as defined above**, not one isolated dip. Apply the **first applicable** response to the next unperformed session; keep unrelated work stable unless global fatigue applies.

| Finding | Action |
|---|---|
| Declining slot has added sets | Remove one added weekly set, newest first |
| Otherwise declining slot has a final 0–1 target | Change that last set to 1–2 RIR |
| T2 keeps declining after both options are exhausted | Remove one WE2 set, once per block; if already used, hold progression and review |
| T3 keeps declining after both options are exhausted | Remove one WE1 set, once per block; if already used, hold progression and review |
| Sunday leg performance keeps declining | Undo one identifiable plausible lower-body dose increase from the last four weeks; if multiple/none plausible, hold and review combined training/recovery |
| Stable plateau or increment too large | Hold; review smaller increment, implement or rep range |

Do not cut the other Wednesday muscle without its own evidence, assume Saturday cycling caused a leg decline, or compensate for removed sets elsewhere.

Log each response and mark its observation window handled. A cut/omission/effort limit persists until documented review. Before another ordinary decline-based reduction, collect **at least two new comparable exposures**, apply the decline test again and retain the same fixed first-set reference if comparable. Never lower the anchor after a cut or reuse handled observations. Hold other discretionary changes for that muscle during observation. Worsening symptoms/clear deterioration or global fatigue may justify earlier action.

## Resolve a decline separately from restoring work

Each persistent-decline case stays open until a documented review of follow-up comparable performance and recovery resolves it. Record evidence, current prescription, recovery/symptoms and why the decline is resolved. Insufficient evidence or continuing problems keep it open and hold dependent progression.

A rollback, handled window, counter/reference reset or manageable week does **not** close a case. A retained local restriction can coexist with a resolved decline; restoring work requires a separate documented review. Likewise global-flag clearance does not silently close cases, and closing one case cannot clear global fatigue.

## Global fatigue

At least three currently unresolved declining **core slots across at least two lifting days**, or reported widespread unusual fatigue persisting into later sessions and compromising normal training, triggers the [persistent global-fatigue response](fatigue.md). A local rollback alone does not resolve a case for this count.

Extra sets are available only through a [deliberate set trial](set-trials.md), never the default response to plateau.
