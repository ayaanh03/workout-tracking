# Assessment weeks

Default optional 5Ks: **W5 (Friday November 27, 2026)** and **W10 (Friday January 1, 2027)**. At most one first-test attempt plus the W10 attempt. The pre-block reported 21:30 does not use either opportunity.

The athlete may deliberately move the first test to **W3 (November 13)** or **W4 (November 20)** after repeated comparable workouts/recovery suggest baseline pace is substantially mismatched. Select before that week's Tuesday session. Record selected week, attempted status and applied overrides. W5 stays light; calendar, ramp, W4/W9 singles and W10 do not move. A single easy workout does not automatically trigger testing.

## Options

| Option | Tuesday | Friday | Wed/Sat bike | Lifting |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A: test | 2 × 4 min at current target | 10 min easy + evenly paced 5K + 5–10 min easy; replaces intervals | ≤15 min each | First-test week: achieved stage; W10: reduced. Omit Wednesday calves. |
| B: no test, recovered | 2 × 4 min at current target | 2 × 6 min at current target | ≤15 min each | W5: achieved stage; W10: reduced. For cancelled early assessment retain already-applied omissions. |
| C: recovery needed, any week | No hard run | No hard run/test | ≤15 comfortable min each, or rest | Reduced from current effective dose under [ramp/return](ramp-and-return.md); use [fatigue response](adjustments.md). |

Normal run warmups/recoveries/cooldowns apply to A Tuesday and B runs. Bike remains after its normal lift. Stricter injury/return/mileage limits always win. No pace/minute progression or ordinary hit/miss/comfortable credit in assessment weeks; targets may slow for effort/safety. Temporary doses preserve normal prescription and successful normal level.

**A eligibility:** athlete wants testing, normally recovered, no worsening symptoms, available opportunity, no active fatigue or illness/gap return restriction. Sole exception: one isolated scheduling miss with gap ≤14 days, confirmed normal current recovery, no worsening symptoms and no other return/fatigue restriction. Test never makes up work; outstanding interval-dose validation remains due.

A test can move to another separate run day within its assessment week, or a race replace it after explicit safe rearrangement under [schedule](schedule.md). Never add a third hard run or combine a test with lifting/cycling. Gym closures mean skip that lift; missed work is not debt.

## Early test decisions

- Selected W3/W4 uses A's endurance doses, achieved-stage lifting, and no Wednesday calves. No separate lifting reduction solely for testing. Apply prospectively, not after Tuesday's ordinary workout.
- Starting the 5K marks the first opportunity **attempted**, even if stopped/compromised. Afterwards W5 is B if recovered, C otherwise; no replacement first test. Wednesday calves return to W5's achieved-stage prescription unless locally restricted.
- If cancelled **before starting**, the unused first opportunity may explicitly revert to W5. Record it. Keep reductions already applied; Friday is B if recovered or C otherwise. Do not restore omitted calves/work later.
- Planned assessments are not missed sessions or automatic return penalties. Real illness, fatigue, skipped work and existing return limits still apply. Assessment doses cannot supply the normal-dose observation needed to clear global fatigue.

## After assessment

Follow [run credit](run-credit.md#assessment-counters-and-carried-award): end early assessment/W5 clears counts and comfortable credit; only an already-saved eligible B carried award can survive. W10 uses its pause/reset rules. Any test attempt cancels both pending awards; a representative result immediately recalibrates targets, while a compromised result does not.

First normal run at reset pace holds no more than the last successful normal minutes (calibration if none). Tests do not validate pending interval-return dose. Friday's 24-minute format remains 3 × 8 before W5 / 4 × 6 from W6. Keep recovery flags, successful normal levels and pending validations.

The Wednesday calf omission does not make an otherwise manageable achieved-stage week incomplete. Post-test recovery still must satisfy advancement gates. An early test does not move or automatically cancel an eligible W4 incline single; normal D and recovery checks decide it.
