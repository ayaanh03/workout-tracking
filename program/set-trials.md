# Deliberate additional-set trial

Default for a stable plateau is **hold and review**, never automatic volume. A documented revision may trial one extra weekly set only when:

- The pattern is a stable plateau, not decline, and no global fatigue flag is active.
- Execution, RIR estimates, adherence, recovery and intake have been reviewed; a large load increment is not the unresolved obstacle.
- Target/slot and fixed prechange first-set reference are recorded.
- That muscle has no unresolved local rollback/cut and no concurrent discretionary increase obscuring the response.

At most one trial at a time; at least two weeks between additions; at most two additional weekly sets per muscle within this block. Priority muscles or incline may be selected. Incline: at most one weekly extra set, **T1 only**, making four there; never add M1a or singles. Calf addition goes to L2. Rear-delt/leg allocations stay fixed; forearms use their separate schedule.

Hold other discretionary changes stable and evaluate at least two comparable exposures. Retain only if tolerated and observed response supports continuation; remove for decline, worsening symptoms or deteriorating recovery. Explicitly adopt/remove before another trial begins. Update effective sets and saved state. A trial does not prove additional growth.

Return A–C suspends trials pending fresh review. Global fatigue cancels unresolved trials; scheduled W10 alone does not. Persistent local ceilings always override a trial. Added sets do not create an extra final 0–1 set: only the new last set can carry that target.
