## Forearm eligibility

Stage A and reduced periods: none. B onward: one set each at 3–4 RIR. At the first normal D week on/after W6, with no fatigue/return restriction, record one elbow-response review. Explicitly no worsening since starting permits two sets each at 2–3 RIR. Yes or uncertain retains one each for this block; review aggravating work. Missing evidence cannot qualify two sets. Symptoms may reduce/omit work sooner. Never failure; load progression needs two qualifying exposures per slot, even with a fixed set count.
