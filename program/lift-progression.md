# Lift loading and progression

Each of the 33 slots has independent load, equipment/setup, actuals and qualification state in [lifting state](../state/lifting.json). M1a/M1b/T1 and all four lateral slots are separate. Historical loads only inform calibration when equipment, notation and setup match; no legacy progression credit transfers. Unknown starting load means **Calibrate**, never an invented weight. Fix the incline angle; use 30° if the original is unknown.

## Ordinary progression

1. Keep load while building reps within the range; stop at the rep ceiling, even if easy.
2. Qualify only when **every prescribed set** reaches the ceiling with stable execution, full set count and intended RIR or more reserve. A final 0–1 target qualifies at 0–1 or more reserve.
3. Lateral raises and cable forearm curls need two qualifying exposures in the same slot. Other lifts need one. Then use the smallest available increment.
4. If that increment prevents the rep floor at intended RIR, use a smaller one if available; otherwise hold the old load and review implement/range. Do not alternate unsuitable loads.
5. A needed mid-session load reduction is logged and disqualifies the original load.

Ramp, reduced, return, changed-equipment and temporarily shortened sessions earn no progression/plateau credit. Global fatigue freezes discretionary progression. Reset qualification streaks after load, set-count or effort-target changes. Preserve a fixed first-set reference if its conditions remain comparable; load or first-set-RIR changes need a new anchor. See [decline rules](adjustments.md).

## Incline slots

- **M1a:** ordinary 4–6 at about 2 RIR. Six clean reps at that reserve qualifies +2.5 lb next Monday, or +5 if 2.5 is unavailable. Calibrate after an increase; never force the floor at excessive effort.
- **M1b:** normally 2×6–8 at 2–3 RIR; stage A has one set. A first calibration estimate is 5–10% below the top-set load; actual effort governs. All prescribed sets at eight qualify the smallest increment. No automatic rise with M1a; back-offs are unchanged on single days.
- **T1:** normally 3×6–8 at 2–3 RIR; stage A has two sets. All prescribed sets at eight qualify the smallest increment, preferably +2.5 lb total. No automatic weekly increase or fourth set.

## Heavy single

W4/W9 only, replacing M1a. Require normal D, no fatigue/return restriction, no relevant worsening symptoms and an appropriate recent comparable M1a estimate. Otherwise retain the ordinary applicable top set or symptom modification; no make-up single. Skip W9 if W4 aggravated a joint and remains unassessed/unresolved.

- Initial planned load: recent comparable M1a e1RM ÷ 1.1, rounded down to 5 lb. Warmup response may lower it.
- Preparation: bar×10, approximately 50%×5, 70%×3 and 85%×1 of planned single; practical loads, no duplicate steps.
- One single at 1–2 RIR. If clearly ≥3 RIR, one further single +5 lb, then stop. The first counts as preparatory work.
- Log **every** heavy exposure; only the last is the counted working set. Keep the ordinary 4–6 record unchanged; resume it next Monday.

## Strength estimate

`e1RM = load × [1 + (completed reps + estimated RIR) / 30]`

Calculate unrounded; display whole pounds. Compare matched reps/RIR/angle/equipment/setup. Singles are separate observations. An estimated 225 lb is not a completed 225 lb single; no maximal single is required. Do not add/remove singles based on a 2% match with the estimate used to choose their load.

## Minimum logging for the next decision

Record dated slot, prescribed versus actual load/reps/RIR for each set, equipment changes, relevant symptoms/recovery, qualification result and next action with source rule. Unknown actuals stay unknown; cached targets cannot supply evidence. Stable plateau means review, not automatic sets; see [adjustments](adjustments.md) and [set trials](set-trials.md).
