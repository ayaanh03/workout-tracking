## 2. Weekly Template (run-priority)

### 2.A Build / Sharpen template (W17–W24) — three-run week (v4.4, from 2026-08-10)
**3 days running, 2 lifting, 2 full off.** Exactly one long, one threshold, one VO2max per week — no fourth run, no easy filler run. Tue and Sat are lift-only; Thu and Sun are permanent off days.

| Day | Session | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | **Long run** (Z1, see §4) | Aerobic volume day. Off Sun's rest; the week's only easy running — keep it genuinely conversational (§4.A) end to end. Non-negotiable: it is the entire easy fraction. *(Post-long stride block removed 2026-08-10, Adj #255.)* |
| **Tue** | **Hypertrophy A** circuit (~45 min) — no run | Lift day. Upper-dominant; 3 days clear of Fri's VO2 |
| **Wed** | **Threshold session** (run or row) — see §4 | Quality day #1. 48 h off Mon's long |
| **Thu** | **Off** — mobility / walk, daily SI prehab | Permanent full rest |
| **Fri** | **VO2max session** (run or row) — see §4 | Quality day #2. 48 h off Wed (unchanged spacing) |
| **Sat** | **Hypertrophy B** circuit (~40 min) including seated calf — **no run** | Lift day. No ≥6 h gap rule any more (nothing to space from); **no Sun fallback — Sun is off, a missed Sat lift is missed** |
| **Sun** | Off, mobility, Tyler twists 3×15 + reverse Tyler 3×15, McGill Big 3 5-3-1 pyramid, daily SI prehab | Walking is fine |

**Structural consequences of v4.4 (all live):**
- **Peak mileage ~20–21 mpw** (was 28). The v4.3 28-mpw ceiling (Adj #67) is no longer binding — it sits above what this template can reach.
- **Every run is load-bearing.** A skipped session costs a third of the week; a skipped Monday leaves the week with *no easy running at all*. There is no punt slot — Sun is off by rule.
- **No lower-body neuromuscular work** (v4.5, Adj #255). Strides were the last remaining dose and are gone; the soleus wall-sit went at W7. Daily SI prehab is durability/glute work, not NM. Accepted trade — see §6.
- **Achilles/calf read moves to Mon AM.** Sat's seated calf sweep is now followed by Sun rest → **Mon's long is the first run after the calf dose (~40 h)**. Any AM-gated calf bump reads on Mon AM, not Sat AM (§10.A watch chain, Adj #211/#230).
- **Sat lift is 24 h after Fri's VO2.** Known-tolerated in the reverse order (Adj #223/#232/#242); watch it in this order.
- **Optional, not programmed:** Sat is now run-free, so a Z1 row (45–60 min, 2:15–2:25/500m, RPE 4–5) can be added there to restore aerobic volume without adding a run or any impact. Precedent: the W16 Mon row, Adj #234/#235.

## 8. Tennis Elbow Rules (§10.A — preserved verbatim)

Goal: keep tendon out of reactive flare while continuing tensile loading.

**Daily:** Tyler twist 3×15 + Reverse Tyler 3×15 (FlexBar, color = 3–4/10 discomfort on eccentric, eccentric phase 4 s).

**Pre-lift:** wrist-extensor isometric 3×30 s each arm.

**Direct biceps work — preacher machine ONLY** until 4 consecutive weeks symptom-free at programmed loads. Free-weight curls (DB hammer, incline DB, BB curl, EZ curl) load the common extensor origin in supination because the wrist must stabilize against the falling DB. Preacher is fixed-path and supports the elbow.

**Direct triceps work — pushdowns + kickbacks ONLY** until 4 weeks symptom-free. Overhead extension forces deep elbow flexion under tensile load with shoulder flexed — stresses medial elbow + forces wrist extensors to stabilize against the rope. Pushdowns keep elbow at side, shorter ROM, smaller wrist arc.

**Re-introduction sequences** (only after 4 wks symptom-free at full programmed loads): see v3.2 §10.A — same protocol, biceps and triceps independently progressed.

**Red flags:** night pain, grip strength drop >20% morning to morning, sharp pain on lockout of pressing → cut accessory volume 50% for 1 week, drop preacher 20%, double Tyler.

**If pain >4/10 on any working set:** drop preacher 20% that session, finish circuit, double Tyler that night.

| **D** (Sharpen+) | **No new exercises**; maintain only what's working | n/a | Drop new adds last 10 days of taper regardless |

### 11.B Scheduled deloads
W8, W16, W20, W24 (every 4 wks — W20 added with the Build extension, Adj #204; W24 embedded in Sharpen). Run volume –30 to –40%, intensity preserved on quality. Lift: same exercises, sets cut 30–50%, intensity 80% of normal.

### 11.C Daily green/yellow/red check (on waking)
- HRV (Apple/Oura) > rolling 7-day avg AND RHR ≤ avg+3 AND SI screen clean → **green** (run as scheduled)
- HRV –1 SD OR RHR avg+4 to +7 OR SI 1–2 positive → **yellow** (downgrade quality day to easy 30–40 min, push quality to next day; **on Mon, trim the long in minutes — it does not move, there is no punt slot**)
- HRV –2 SD OR RHR >avg+7 OR SI 3+ positive OR walking pain >3/10 → **red** (off, mobility, reschedule)

### 11.D Weekly volume bump rule
≤10% mileage increase wk-to-wk during ramp; never increase mileage AND quality complexity in same week. Never add a new lower-body exercise the same week as a mileage bump.

### 11.E Accutane recovery layer
- Hydration: ≥3.5 L lift days, ≥4.5 L long-run days, ≥4.5 L race week. Track.
- No sauna, no extreme heat. Limit summer long runs to <70°F if possible — start at 6 AM in Jul/Aug.
- Watch for: muscle pain >72 h, dark urine, persistent unilateral weakness, calf cramping out of proportion.
- **Trigger CK lab draw** if any of above. Hold all lifting + reduce running 50% until CK <500 U/L. Documented isotretinoin-exercise rhabdo cases: PMC9287672, PMC8857541, PMC7660121 — rare but real.
- Mention to dermatologist that the program ramps 0 → ~21 mpw + lifting (was 0 → 28 pre-v4.4); ask about baseline CK.
- No vitamin A supps. No tetracyclines. Chalk on barbell (skin tear risk).

### 11.F Sleep & nutrition floor
Sleep 7.5–8 h. Protein 1.6–1.8 g/kg. Carbs 0.5–1 g/kg pre, 1+ g/kg post on hard days. **More carbs once running mileage hits 20+ mpw** — under-fueled long runs is the #1 mistake at sub-20 mileage, and at the v4.3 lower ceiling the fuelling floor matters *more*, not less, for protecting muscle (goal 2).
